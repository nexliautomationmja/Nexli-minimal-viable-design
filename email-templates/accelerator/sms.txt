Accelerator delivery - the two SMS
==================================

Same branch as the emails: funnel_path = "agency" or "not-a-fit".
Send alongside the email, not instead of it - SMS cannot carry the PDF,
so its job is to tell them the inbox has it and, on the qualified
branch, to carry the one link worth clicking.

ASCII only. Type apostrophes and hyphens directly rather than pasting
from a doc: a smart quote drops the message out of GSM-7 encoding and
segments go from 153 characters to 67, so the same text silently costs
three times as much to send.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. QUALIFIED   funnel_path = "agency"   (send with 1-qualified.txt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

{{inboundWebhookRequest.firstName}}, your Accelerator just landed in
your inbox.

Your firm also cleared the bar for the done-for-you version - we fill
your calendar with $15K to $55K planning clients:

{{inboundWebhookRequest.resume_url}}

Reply STOP to opt out.


~235 characters, 2 segments.

The link is the whole point of this one. It opens /accelerator/next
signed in - the walkthrough and the calendar - so the SMS does the job
the email's button does, for the half of people who never open email on
a phone.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. NOT QUALIFIED   funnel_path = "not-a-fit"   (send with 2-free-lead.txt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

{{inboundWebhookRequest.firstName}}, your Book of Business Accelerator
is in your inbox. Free, nothing to click.

Page three is where it starts: twenty clients, four conversations,
$60,000.

Reply STOP to opt out.


~195 characters, 2 segments.

NO LINK, deliberately. resume_url on this branch opens
/accelerator/sending, which is a delivery confirmation with nothing on
it - a dead end. And no agency pitch either: the email plants that as a
later conversation, and repeating it in a text to someone who was just
told they are not a fit is pushing. Delivery, one instruction, done.
