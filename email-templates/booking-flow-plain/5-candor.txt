Every firm owner should get a straight answer to these five questions
before hiring any marketing agency, us included:

1. What does it cost every month, and what does that cover?
2. How long am I locked in?
3. If I leave, who owns the website and everything you built?
4. What happens if it doesn't work?
5. I've been burned before. Why would this be any different?

If an agency gets vague on any of them, that's your answer.
