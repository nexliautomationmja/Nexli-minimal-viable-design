Booked Appointment Flow - the Candor SMS
=========================================

Sent with 5-candor.txt, in the same workflow step. The email makes the
case; the text is the one most people actually open, on the phone they
will tap the link from.

Its job is not to sell. It is to get the doubt they would not say on the
call - the thing that would stop them hiring any agency - out of their
head and into Candor before the call, where Marcel reads it.

ASCII only. Type apostrophes and hyphens directly rather than pasting
from a doc: a smart quote or an em dash drops the message out of GSM-7
encoding and segments go from 153 characters to 67, so the same text
silently costs three times as much to send.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Hi {{contact.first_name}}, it's Marcel at Nexli. Before you hire any agency, us included, every doubt you have should be answered. Especially the ones you'd rather not say on the call - price, getting burned before, timing:

{{contact.candor_url}}

Reply STOP to opt out.


~289 characters with an 11-letter first name, once candor_url renders
(~51 characters: https://www.nexli.net/candor?c=<20-char contact id>).
2 segments (306 is the cap). A first name over ~27 letters tips it to
3. If you edit the copy, recount against the rendered link, not the tag.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT IS WORDED THIS WAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------

"us included" - the line is about agencies in general, which only works
if Nexli is not exempting itself. It is what makes the ask read as
candid rather than as a pitch.

"the ones you'd rather not say on the call" - the same promise the page
makes ("Type the thing you would not say on the call..."). The doubts
named after it are the page's own buttons, so they recognise theirs and
tap it instead of typing.

What it does NOT say, on purpose:
  - No privacy promise. Marcel gets every conversation. The page dropped
    "nothing you ask comes up on the call" for that reason.
  - No "Daniela will answer you". Candor is an AI and the page says so;
    the text must not imply a person is on the other end.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
SETUP IN GHL
--------------------------------------------------------------------

When:     same step as 5-candor.txt - between the confirmation and the
          48-hour email. If the call is under ~48h away, send it right
          after the confirmation rather than skipping it; that is when
          the doubts are loudest.

Consent:  gate the step on marketing_sms_opt_in = true
          (docs/advisory-funnel-launch.md - TCPA, not preference). This
          one persuades, so it is not covered by the transactional
          reminder consent.

The link: {{contact.candor_url}} is a contact custom field ("Candor
          URL", key candor_url). Create it once in GHL: Settings > Custom
          Fields > Contact > Single line. The site writes it when they
          book - /candor?c=<their GHL contact id> - which is what puts a
          name on the conversation: phones that tap a text link carry no
          funnel cookie, so without it the transcript arrives anonymous.
          A contact field, not a webhook value, so it shows up in the
          variable picker under Contact whatever starts the workflow.
          Do NOT write /candor?c={{contact.id}} by hand - GHL's editor
          rejects a merge tag inside a URL ("issues in your custom
          variables").

Name:     {{contact.first_name}}, not {{inboundWebhookRequest.firstName}} -
          also a contact field, so it never renders blank.

Then, in the Candor transcript email workflow (GHL_CANDOR_WEBHOOK_URL),
add above the transcript:

    Contact: {{inboundWebhookRequest.contact_url}}

That is a one-click link to the contact record, built by the site from
the id. It is blank when the conversation came from a link without ?c=.
