What running it the other way round looks like

The old way

Compliance. Payroll. Bookkeeping. Planning quietly baked in, unbilled, somewhere in the middle of filing season.

The new way

The planning engagement is the anchor fee and the front door. Compliance, payroll and bookkeeping become the recurring layer that keeps the client with you.

Why it holds

A client who pays you $15,000 to restructure their position does not shop their $1,200 return next year. The advisory engagement is what makes the compliance work sticky, not the other way round.

Why this has to be now

Most planning moves have to be executed inside the tax year. Sell an engagement in November and your client sees the saving on the return you file in April. Sell the same one in February and the year is already written.

You do not need new clients to do this. You need to change the order you sell in.
